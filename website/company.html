WHY ONE SHAPE

If you can't glance it, you can't govern it.

Every department gets one visual, three numbers, one lever, one leak. Same shape, domain words. The same eye that scans Finance can scan Risk without translation — and the same dispatcher can approve a Pause on intake and a Lock on the FX hedge without switching contexts. That is what the company becomes when it is treated as a system, not a P&L.
