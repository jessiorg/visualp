A VIRTUAL COMPANY · PCO LIFECYCLE

The operating activities of a virtual company.

A PCO is a persistent operating twin for a real enterprise. It keeps the same balance sheet, missions, risks, and reinvestment grammar as the underlying business — but exposes them as surfaces you can observe and act on.

FIVE STAGES · IN ORDER

The PCO lifecycle, drawn as a path.

STAGE 01

Statement drop

Provide the minimum statement of current reality — what the company does, what it owns, what it owes, who depends on it.

OutputsBaseline state
Form factorOnboarding form

STAGE 02

Virtual twin activation

Infer a useful operating baseline without demanding a complete data model. The twin starts alive, then grows with evidence.

OutputsPCO identity
Form factorTwin record

STAGE 03

Zero-risk outcome activation

Begin with an outcome whose downside is bounded and measurable. No autonomy is granted until evidence supports it.

OutputsActivation switch
Form factorApproval queue

STAGE 04

Customer sale & recurring relation

Convert demonstrated value into a paid, recurring relationship. Each renewal is itself an evidence event written back to the twin.

OutputsRevenue event
Form factorSettlement record

STAGE 05

Reinvestment & spawn

Liquid FCF is allocated to high-ROCE physical assets. Proven patterns spawn new PCO twins for adjacent units.

OutputsCapex event
Form factorReinvestment record

CONTINUOUS

Two-surface operating loop

After activation, Pulse and Dispatch run continuously. Pulse detects gaps; Dispatch creates missions; settlement writes evidence back.

Loop cadenceHourly · daily
Autonomy levelBounded by evidence

HOW IT OPERATES

Pulse detects. Dispatch acts. Evidence writes back.

SURFACE A · PULSE

Detect

Read-only state surface. The twin's current truth, continuously refreshed from telemetry and settlement feeds.

  • Balance sheet snapshot
  • Survival floor (30 / 90 d)
  • Free cash flow ticker
  • CCE trend (rolling 90 d)
  • Unhedged risk register
  • Mission outcomes & counterparty health

SURFACE B · DISPATCH

Act

Action surface. Bounded missions, approval queues, wholesale block aggregation, settlement evidence.

  • Mission author & approver
  • Spending limit & escalation rule
  • Wholesale block aggregation queue
  • Settlement record & counterparty ack
  • Audit log & rollback / hold state
  • Evidence written back to Pulse

Autonomy is bounded by permissions, spending limits, escalation rules, audit logs, and rollback / hold states. A recommendation is not authority.
