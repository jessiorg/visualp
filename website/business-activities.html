BUSINESS ACTIVITIES · CASH IN

The money that comes in for what we sell.

If the company page is the body — eight departments, eight functions — this page is the bloodstream. Where the revenue comes from, in what shape, at what margin, and how much of it returns as liquid free cash flow that the company can deploy.

THE CASH-IN FORMULA

price × volume × mix × collection rate

Four knobs, one number. Move any one and the answer changes. Move all four in the same direction and the company is on a different trajectory within a quarter. Move none of them and the company is just running.
