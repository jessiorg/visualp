BUSINESS ACTIVITIES

The cashflow grammar under the chrome.

Operating activities generate outcomes. Business activities turn those outcomes into liquid free cash flow, then deploy that cash into high-ROCE physical assets. The cycle widens.

NORTH-STAR · PCO

Cash Conversion Efficiency.

CCE is the share of operating profit that actually becomes liquid free cash flow. The planning target is greater than 80%. Do not substitute revenue growth or EBITDA margin for CCE.

FORMULA

CCE = FCF / Gross Operating Profit

Define the reporting period. Reconcile operating profit to cash. Explain working capital, prepayments, exceptional items, and attribution.

DriverImpact

REINVESTMENT FLYWHEEL

Liquid FCF → physical assets → wider spreads → more FCF.

The cycle only compounds while each link actually moves. Treat it as a measurement loop, not a slogan.
