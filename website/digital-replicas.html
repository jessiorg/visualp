DIGITAL REPLICA · THE PATTERN

Same shape, different business.

A digital replica is a bounded scenario built from the same Pulse + Dispatch grammar. Sim-Refinery, Sim-LAES, and any new venture follow the same template: state surface, action surface, settlement evidence, and a scrubbable scenario timeline.

REPLICA TEMPLATE · FOUR SURFACES

Every replica exposes the same four surfaces.

The grammar is fixed. The numbers, asset classes, and counterparty maps change per replica.

01 · PULSE

State surface

Live balance sheet, survival floor, FCF ticker, CCE trend.

02 · DISPATCH

Action surface

Missions, agents, approval queue, wholesale blocks.

03 · EVIDENCE

Settlement record

Counterparty, amount, timestamp, evidence artefacts.

04 · TIMELINE

Scenario scrubber

Bounded scenario with play / pause / scrub. Evidence flows in order.
