VALUE TAXONOMY · LOCKED

Every metric, mission, and replica is tagged.

The seven-tier model is a progression from individual / household economic reality through health, environment, and enterprise outcomes, with monetisation across every tier. There is no eighth tier.

THE SEVEN TIERS

Each tier names a class of outcome.

Both P1 and PCO can create value in any tier. They differ in which tier they primarily express.

01
Energy and power
Wholesale energy blocks, internal LCOE displacement, baseload supply. The base of every operating unit.
P1 · PCO
02
Shelter and facilities
Plant maintenance, occupancy margin, site consolidation, working conditions for the people inside.
PCO
03
Health, biology, biomarkers
Visceral fat, sleep, metabolic signals, biomarker tracking. Outcome deltas, not diagnostic or treatment claims.
P1
04
Climate and regeneration
Scope 1–3, pollution reduction, clean-energy offtake, externalities priced in or hedged out.
P1 · PCO
05
Nutrition and COGS
Wholesale procurement of staples, COGS reduction at the household and site level, biomethane feedstock.
P1 · PCO
06
Mobility and logistics
Aggregated freight, PCO site consolidation, supply continuity, last-mile coordination.
PCO
07
Capital, treasury, identity
Ownership, cash conversion, treasury yield, Nostr / DuckDB / x402 as implementation candidates — not commitments.
P1 · PCO

THE RULE

Every feature, metric, or diagram identifies its tier.

No eighth tier. No silent renames. Implementation candidates are tagged as such, never as commitments.

P1 · PCO ISOMORPHISM

Different subjects, shared grammar.

Both P1 (virtual person / household) and PCO (virtual company) maintain balance sheet, P&L, commitments, risks, constraints, missions, evidence, settlement, and reinvestment. The difference is the subject of the twin — never the control logic.
