# Seven-tier value taxonomy

The taxonomy is a value map for both P1 people/households and PCO companies. Each tier can generate outcomes, evidence and monetisation; none should be treated as a promise without measurement and consent.

1. Energy: supply, procurement, clean-energy offtake, demand shaping and internal LCOE.
2. Shelter and plant: buildings, equipment, occupancy, maintenance and productive physical capacity.
3. Health and vitality: sleep, metabolic markers, visceral fat, biological age, longevity and no-additives nutrition. Biomarker tracking requires explicit consent and medical boundaries; the system is not a diagnostic service.
4. Climate and externalities: Scope 1–3 reduction, local pollution, avoided damage, community externalities and clean-energy displacement.
5. Nutrition and COGS: food and industrial inputs, quality, waste, procurement and cost of goods sold.
6. Mobility and logistics: routing, freight, transport utilisation, aggregation and settlement.
7. Capital and identity: cash conversion, reinvestment, ownership and programmable identity/settlement. Nostr, DuckDB and x402 are implementation candidates, not mandatory dependencies.

P1 prioritises wealth floor, non-wage income, vitality and lived wellbeing. PCO prioritises margin expansion, resilience, workforce vitality and ecological handprint. WELLBY and CCE provide distinct north stars across the same taxonomy.
