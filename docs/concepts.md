# P1 / PCO concepts

## P1: virtual person
A P1 is a persistent economic and operational twin for a person or household. It observes a statement of needs, constraints, inflows, outflows, health and vitality signals, and translates them into an actionable sovereign economic plan.

## PCO: virtual company
A PCO is the organisational counterpart: a persistent operating twin for a company, team, site, or supply-chain unit. It turns operational data into missions, risk controls, margin improvements, resilience, and measurable outcomes.

## Isomorphism
P1 and PCO share the same abstract lifecycle: identity and baseline state, constraints, target outcomes, dispatchable missions, execution evidence, cash-flow feedback, and reinvestment. The difference is the subject of the twin, not the control logic.

## Occam's razor onboarding
1. Statement drop: provide the minimum statement of current reality.
2. Virtual twin activation: infer a useful baseline without demanding a complete data model.
3. Zero-risk outcome activation: begin with an outcome whose downside is bounded and measurable.
4. Customer sale: convert demonstrated value into a paid, recurring relationship.

The principle is to remove setup friction: start from an observable statement and earn progressively richer data.

## Two surfaces
Surface A — Sovereign Pulse: a calm, continuously updated view of survival floor, savings, inflows, outflows, health/vitality and leading indicators.

Surface B — Dispatch: the execution cockpit. It shows agents, active missions, blockers, owners, expected value, evidence, and next actions. Pulse describes state; Dispatch changes state.
