# WELLBY and CCE metrics

## WELLBY for P1
WELLBY expresses a change in wellbeing associated with life satisfaction and healthy life years. A defensible implementation needs a baseline, a defined intervention, consented measures, time horizon, attribution assumptions and uncertainty. A planning range of £150–£250 per WELLBY appeared in the discussion, but it is an assumption for modelling, not a universal price.

P1 optimisation must not maximise cash while degrading health, autonomy or lived experience. Report outcome delta, confidence, participant-reported experience and distributional effects.

## CCE for PCO
Cash conversion efficiency is:

`CCE = free cash flow / gross operating profit`

The planning north star is above 80%. Define the period, normalise exceptional items, reconcile operating profit to cash, and distinguish customer prepayments, working-capital timing and genuine recurring conversion. CCE is not revenue growth and not EBITDA margin.

WELLBY and CCE are analogous outcome measures, not interchangeable ones: the first describes human flourishing, the second deployable enterprise cash. Both feed mission selection and reinvestment.
